- **Task, project and question ids in a terminal are links.** An id such as `T-1a2b`, `P-5e6f` or
  `N-0000abcd` printed in a terminal is a link when the tracker knows it: hovering shows its title,
  and clicking opens the Projects view on the task's sheet, the project, or the question. Other text
  stays text. See
  [The Projects view](docs/features.md#the-projects-view-and-the-project-tracker).
