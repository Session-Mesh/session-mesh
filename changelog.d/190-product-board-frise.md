- **A product has its own Board and Timeline.** A product's view in Projects now has three tabs:
  Overview (its projects and child products, as before), Board and Timeline, which show every task
  of the product and of its child products, each card with its project and the Timeline grouped
  by project. Filters by project, status, priority, owner, tag and text apply to both tabs and are
  kept with the view's place. The open columns add up to the product's count in the sidebar; a
  product with more than 1000 tasks says its view is partial. See
  [the Projects view](docs/features.md#the-projects-view-and-the-project-tracker).
