- **Links and documents in the Projects view.** A web address in a question, answer, note, blocker
  or event, or in a task's links, opens in a new tab, and a document path such as
  `~/notes/plan.html` opens the document in a panel beside the view (full screen on a phone).
  HTML and SVG pages show their styles and inline diagrams, never their scripts. The panel shows
  only what the document panel may show. See
  [The Projects view](docs/features.md#the-projects-view-and-the-project-tracker).
