- **Projects lists its products and projects in the left sidebar.** While the Projects view is
  shown, the sidebar holds a tree of zones, products and projects with their open tasks and open
  questions; choosing a product shows its view, choosing a project opens it, and the arrow keys
  move through the tree. The entry screen keeps the pending questions and an overview of the
  products. The unfolded items are kept with the rest of the view's place for the tab; on a
  phone the tree is in the drawer, which closes on a choice. See
  [the Projects view](docs/features.md#the-projects-view-and-the-project-tracker).
