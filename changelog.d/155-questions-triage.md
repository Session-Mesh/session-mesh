- **The Projects view opens on the products again; its open questions are one folded line,
  classified, and answered in context.** The entry screen no longer starts with every open
  question and its answer form: a folded line "Pending questions n" sits above the products, and
  the browser tab remembers whether it is unfolded. Unfolded, it lists the questions by zone (the
  category of the product), product, project and task, with counts, the oldest first; a row gives
  the text, a "Decision" cue when the agent offered options, its age and asker, where it stands,
  the options and the assumption (folded), and opens the task sheet at that question (or the
  project's Questions tab, the sheet of a task in no project, or the question's own sheet), where
  the answer is given beside the task's notes, blockers and events; Back returns to the list.
  Questions attached to no task are grouped under "Without context" by the session that asked
  them. `work-read resume` adds a `context` to each question (task title, project, product,
  category), and the `mesh-work` instructions and the agent guide ask agents to attach each
  question to the task or project it concerns. See
  [The Projects view and the project tracker](docs/features.md#the-projects-view-and-the-project-tracker).
