- **The Projects view opens on the products again; its open questions are one folded line,
  classified, and answered in context.** The entry screen no longer starts with every open
  question and its answer form: a folded line "Pending questions n" sits above the products, and
  the browser tab remembers whether it is unfolded, with the rest of the view's place.
  Unfolded, it lists the questions by zone (the category of the product), product, project and
  task, with counts, the oldest first; a row gives the text, a "Decision" cue when the agent
  offered options, its age and asker, where it stands, the options and the assumption (folded),
  and opens the task sheet at that question (or the project's Questions tab, the sheet of a task
  in no project, or the question's own sheet), where the answer is given beside the task's notes,
  blockers and events; Back returns to the list. Questions attached to no task are grouped under
  "Without context" by the session that asked them. Leaving Projects and coming back, or
  reloading the tab, reopens a sheet opened from the list with the answer typed there; signing
  out forgets it, the unfolded line included. `work-read resume` adds a `context` to each
  question (task title, project, product, category), and the `mesh-work` instructions and the
  agent guide ask agents to attach each question to the task or project it concerns. See
  [The Projects view and the project tracker](docs/features.md#the-projects-view-and-the-project-tracker).
