- **The Projects view comes back as the owner left it, unsent answers included.** Leaving
  Projects (the Sessions button, a session, `Esc`, a link) and coming back, or
  reloading the tab, shows the same project, tab, task sheet and scroll, read again, with every
  answer typed but not yet sent, in any sheet or tab. The drafts stay in the
  browser tab only (`sessionStorage`, bounded), are never sent until the answer is, and are
  removed once it is recorded, when the field is emptied, and when the browser signs out or its
  credential is refused. See
  [The Projects view](docs/features.md#the-projects-view-and-the-project-tracker).
