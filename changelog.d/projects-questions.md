- **Projects shows the open questions of agents and lets you answer them where you see them.** The
  first screen lists the open questions, "Without a task" and "About a task"; each project has a
  Questions tab grouped by task; both carry the answer form of the task sheet. The Projects button
  shows how many questions are open. See
  [The Projects view](docs/features.md#the-projects-view-and-the-project-tracker).
