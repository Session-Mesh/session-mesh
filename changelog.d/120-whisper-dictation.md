- **Voice dictation through a configurable Whisper-compatible endpoint.** Hold the microphone in
  a local Claude Code, Codex or Pi terminal header. Desktop transcripts enter the original prompt
  without Enter; phones stage an editable draft bound to its original pane and session incarnation
  for manual Send. Capture, upload and recovery are bounded, uncertain audio is never resent, and
  recovery asks before replacing a saved draft. The relay is opt-in; remote endpoints require HTTPS
  and loopback HTTP is supported. See [Voice dictation](docs/features.md#voice-dictation) and
  [configuration](docs/operations.md#whisper-dictation).
