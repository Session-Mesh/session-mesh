- **The Sessions view shows which sessions have open questions.** A session with open questions
  (asked by it, or about a task it works on or that task's project) shows "? n" on its row and a
  "? n" button in its header, which opens the questions in Projects with their answer form. See
  [The Projects view](docs/features.md#the-projects-view-and-the-project-tracker).
