# session-mesh Contributor License Agreement

This file holds the agreement that every contributor to session-mesh accepts, and the record of
its versions. You accept it by adding your line to [CLA-SIGNATORIES.md](CLA-SIGNATORIES.md) in a
commit of your own; [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#contributor-license-agreement) gives the
steps. Its section 12 binds the maintainers among themselves.

**This agreement was not reviewed by a lawyer.** The maintainers wrote it, drawing on widely used
agreements: the Apache Software Foundation Individual Contributor License Agreement, the Harmony
Contributor Agreements and the Canonical Contributor License Agreement. It does not choose a
governing law or a jurisdiction. If you or your employer need a reviewed agreement before
contributing, say so in an issue.

## Versions

Each version is frozen once published: its text never changes. A new version is added as a new
dated section of this file, above the previous ones, and contributions made after it ask for
acceptance of the new version. Acceptances keep the version they name.
